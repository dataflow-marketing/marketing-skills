# Ask an AI: a whole conversation

What happens when the coffee shop's [ask-ai-copy.txt](ask-ai-copy.txt) is pasted into an AI chat, as it is. You can read it here before trying it yourself.

The coffee shop, its owner and the owner's answers are made up, like everything in this example. The AI's replies were written by Claude from the copy alone, following the instructions at its top. Another AI (or the same one on another day) will word things differently; the shape should be the same.

---

**The owner:** *(pastes the whole of ask-ai-copy.txt and sends it)*

**The AI:**

You started 6 and then 9 stamp cards a week, an average of 7.5, against your goal of 10 a week by 31 December. The forecast says 10 is likely this week. It rests on only 2 counted weeks: it is rough. So far this week (to 2 December) you have 4.

1. Top 3 quick wins
2. The biggest gap: you offer a card with every first coffee, yet 15 of 2,550 visits started one (believed)
3. Help with this week's market stall, not done yet
4. Which unknown link to count next
5. Something else

---

Three things you could do this week, each from what you already have:

1. Stamp cards at the market stall on 3 December: the stall is already in your plan this week. The cards just go with you.
2. A post about the stamp card: your stamp card page already says "The tenth coffee is on us."
3. A line at the counter: your home page already says "Ask for a stamp card with your first coffee."

4. Something else

One Instagram post about the stamp card, done once, this week. It should lead to more stamp cards started. How many is not known yet: a post has never been counted against stamp cards.

This week's new post is done (Iced coffee is back): this would be an extra post. Your words so far: "Ask for a stamp card with your first coffee." and "The tenth coffee is on us."

1. Use those two lines as they are

**The owner:** 2. We see the same faces every morning. This is our thank you.

**The AI:**

Round 4 of 7

Here is your response. Copy it into an email to hello@coffee-shop.example with the subject Changes to my plan:

```
Change: 2026-11-30 + stamp card post
Change: how stamp card post: Post the stamp card on Instagram in our own words.
Change: stamp card post -> stamp cards started: ? (unknown)
Change: script stamp card post: We see the same faces every morning. This is our thank you. Ask for a stamp card with your first coffee. The tenth coffee is on us.

Why: one extra post this week, from words we already use, to see whether it starts more stamp cards.
```

The post, ready to use:

> We see the same faces every morning. This is our thank you.
> Ask for a stamp card with your first coffee. The tenth coffee is on us.

Nothing is posted or changed until you do it. Would you like to look at another option?

1. The market stall on 3 December
2. Which unknown link to count next
3. Something else

---

## What to notice

- **Every number came from the summary:** 6, 9, 7.5, 4 and 15 of 2,550. The AI worked none out itself.
- **Not known is said as not known:** the post's effect on stamp cards is `?`, never a guess.
- **The words are the owner's:** two lines from their own site and one line they wrote. The AI wrote no new claims.
- **It never says anything was sent:** the owner posts it and sends the changes.
- **The ending is the response:** `Change:` lines in the plan's own words. With the skills, `/dfm-ledger:plan-change` takes them back into the plan after a yes.
