# The starter plan

Every business starts on the same plan: [ledger.txt](ledger.txt). It needs no website and no tools. It is a loop of four steps a week in order, with a goal of one more follower a week. It is the plan dataflow marketing starts everyone on.

| Step | What it involves |
|---|---|
| check your profiles | Opening times, contact details and what you offer are up to date. Many businesses let this slip. |
| new post | Answer a question a customer asked you this week, in a few lines. |
| post once on social | Use content from your new post. |
| reply to comments | Reply to everyone who comments, within a day. Count the comments. |

Two things are counted, both told by the person: followers (a total) and comments (each week). Every link in the graph is `?`: nothing is known yet. That is the point. The loop is how it becomes known.

## Start

1. Copy [ledger.txt](ledger.txt).
2. Change `start` and `started` to the Monday of your first week.
3. Put your followers today as the first `fact total` line.

## Iterate

The starter plan is where everyone begins, not where they stay. Each week:

1. **Do the steps** and tick them in done.
2. **Count:** your followers on the day and your comments for the week.
3. **Summarise** and look at the [forecast](../../spec/forecast.md): after two counted weeks it can give a rough date for the goal.
4. **Ask an AI** with the [tutorial](../../docs/ask-an-ai.md). Pick "Top 3 quick wins" or look at the biggest gap.
5. **Change one thing.** Keep the `Change:` lines you want and add them to your ledger. One change a week keeps it clear which change did what.

How plans usually grow from here:

- **The goal changes** to what the business actually wants a week: enquiries, bookings, sales, sign ups.
- **Facts are added** for what can now be counted: site visits, emails sent and opened, enquiries.
- **Steps are added** for the business: an email to the list, a referral ask, a script for a message.
- **Links get rates** as counts arrive. They move from `?` to believed to proven.
- **Groups appear** when it matters who responds: regulars, new customers, schools.

The [coffee shop](../coffee-shop/README.md) shows where a plan can be a few weeks later.
